Second Symposium on Analytic Number Theory

Cetraro, 8-12 July 2019

Registration

The costs of the rooms (including all meals except the banquet and pizza night) were as follows:

  • 110 Euro/day for a single room,
  • 96 Euro/day per person for a double room (please indicate if the other occupant will be another participant),
  • 80 Euro/day per person for a room in residences accommodating 3 to 4 people.
For the single/double rooms precedence was given to more senior mathematicians.
There was also a supplement charge of 42 Euro for the pizza and banquet. The hotel also provided a shuttle service between the Lamezia Terme airport or the Paola train station and the hotel. See the location tab for more details.
