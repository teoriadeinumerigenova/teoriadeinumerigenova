Second Symposium on Analytic Number Theory

Cetraro, 8-12 July 2019

Second Symposium on Analytic Number Theory

Cetraro, 8-12 July 2019

Organizers: D. Bazzanella, S. Bettin, A. Perelli

The conference Second Symposium on Analytic Number Theory took place in the beautiful setting of the Grand Hotel San Michele in Cetraro, a town in the region of Calabria in the south of Italy.

The conference was funded by the Politecnico of Torino, the University of Genova, the Foundation Compositio Mathematica and the Istituto Nazionale di Alta Matematica "Francesco Severi" - INdAM.

For any information please contact us at symposium.ant@gmail.com.
