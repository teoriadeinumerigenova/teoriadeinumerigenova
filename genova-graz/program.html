Program

All seminars will take place in Room 704 (half a floor up from the main entrance, on the right side).

Monday May 30

  • 13.45 - 14: Welcome & Coffee
  • 14.00 - 14.45: F. Veneziano (Università di Genova): Rational angles between points in a plane lattice
  • 15.00 - 15.45: S. Chaubey (Indraprastha Institute of Information Technology Delhi): Zeros of the Derivatives of the Selberg class L functions
  • 19.00: Conference dinner

Tuesday May 31

  • 9.45 - 10: Coffee
  • 10.00 - 10.45: B. Borda (Graz University of Technology): Randomness of Sudler products
  • 11.00 - 11.45: S. Drappeau (Université d'Aix-Marseille): Quantum modularity for central values of additively twisted Maass L-functions
  • 12.00 - 13.45: Lunch Break
    13.45 - 14.00: Coffee

  • 15.00 - 15.45: A. Zafeiropoulos (Norwegian University of Science and Technology): Poissonian correlations: higher orders and weaker variants.

Wednesday June 1

  • 9.45 - 10: Coffee
  • 10.00 - 10.45: D. El-Baz (Graz University of Technology): Primitive rational points on expanding horospheres: effective joint equidistribution
  • 11.00 - 11.45: E. Carneiro (International Centre for Theoretical Physics): Some Fourier optimization problems in number theory
  • 12.00 - 13.45: Lunch Break
    13.45 - 14.00: Coffee
  • 14.00 - 14.45: J. Lee (University of Warwick): Another view of Ferrero-Washington Theorem
  • 15.00 - 15.45: M. Munsch (Università di Genova): Zeros of Fekete polynomials and applications
